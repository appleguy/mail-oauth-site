Privacy policy

Effective: October 10, 2026.

Personal Mail Tools is a private application used by its owner to access and organize that owner's Gmail account. No public user registration is offered.

Data accessed

With the Google account owner's authorization, local tools may access Gmail messages, senders, recipients, subject lines, conversation identifiers, message bodies, attachments when requested, and Gmail labels. Initial CLI access is read-only. If the owner later enables inbox-organization features, the application may also request permission to change labels or archive messages.

How data is used

Gmail data is used only to perform the account owner's requested search, review, classification, and organization tasks. It is not sold, used for advertisements, or used to train advertising profiles. This site itself neither receives nor hosts Gmail data.

Storage, sharing, and retention

Local tools may store authentication tokens and selected email data on devices controlled by the owner. Such local data is retained only for the owner's tasks and may be deleted locally. Google data is not shared with independent third-party processors as part of the current initial local configuration. Before any separate hosted AI provider or managed Gmail tool is activated, this policy must be updated to accurately disclose that processing and retention.

Owner control and deletion

The owner can revoke Google access through Google Account settings, stop the local tools, and delete any local email snapshots and stored credentials. Where an optional local Inbox Zero installation is enabled, its own database and retention configuration are controlled by the owner.

Limited use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
